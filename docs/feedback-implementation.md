# Feedback implementation map

This note records how the recommendations in **DEBRIEFING SITE WEB** and
**MISE À JOUR DU SITE YAHNU** are represented in the product. It is also a
guardrail for future changes.

## Product interpretation

Yahnu's public front door is an institutional impact story. Its authenticated
workspaces are the operational proof of that story. Job discovery is therefore
one workflow inside Yahnu—not the whole identity of the platform.

The product must never invent partners, outcomes, testimonials or employer
confirmation. Pilot targets are labelled as targets; published results must
come from measured platform data.

## Recommendation trace

| Feedback theme | Product response |
| --- | --- |
| Explain Yahnu in ten seconds | Editorial hero, pilot narrative and clear audience calls to action on the homepage |
| Make **BE THE CHANGE** the strategic spine | Dedicated `/be-the-change` page, shared-responsibility narrative and site-wide language |
| Speak to each audience directly | Dedicated `/students`, `/schools`, `/companies` and `/institutions` journeys |
| Show transformation, not partner lists | Pilot journey, role-specific workspaces, consented organization profiles, events, applications and outcome reporting |
| Use measurable impact | `/impact` and `/methodologie` explain targets, definitions, measurement cadence and limitations |
| Show success stories | CMS-backed journal and impact content; no fabricated testimonials or case studies |
| Provide real opportunities | Current official employer-source examples on the public site and automated official ATS discovery after graduate sign-in |
| Provide credible education references | Non-ranked, non-partner directory of Côte d'Ivoire institutions linking only to official websites |
| Use contextual calls to action | Audience, opportunity, institution, contact and pilot actions lead to the relevant journey |
| Make content operational | Admin/content workspaces manage journal content, static pages, job sources and moderation |
| Treat logos carefully | No organization is described as a partner merely because it appears in a reference or source list; public organization publication requires explicit consent |
| Be institutional, factual and mobile-first | Restrained motion, semantic hierarchy, responsive layouts, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and plain-language disclosures |
| Prepare post-launch operations | Support workspace, announcements, knowledge base, reports, job-source health and editable content |

## Truth and trust rules

- “Verified” always states what was verified and when.
- External job applications are candidate-declared unless an employer confirms
  them through Yahnu.
- A Yahnu Skills Check is an attestation under stated conditions, not a diploma
  or accredited certification.
- The first version uses no camera, microphone, screen recording or human
  monitoring.
- Public school and company profiles require explicit publication consent and
  minimum completeness.
- Institution references do not imply ranking, endorsement or partnership.
- External links must be HTTPS and point to an approved official employer,
  institution or ATS host.
